A SMARTER FIRST SEMESTER

Set yourself up for success.

  • Before classes
  • Each week
  • Before exams

Know your schedule.

  • Check room numbers and building locations.
  • Read each syllabus and note major due dates.
  • Introduce yourself to an instructor or advisor.

Build consistency.

  • Use a calendar for classes, deadlines, and study blocks.
  • Review notes for 15–20 minutes after class.
  • Ask questions in office hours when something is unclear.

Study actively.

  • Practice with sample problems and self-quizzes.
  • Start early rather than cramming the night before.
  • Use tutoring, study groups, and structured breaks.
REMEMBER

Advising is a conversation.

Your interests, previous credit, program, and academic goals all matter. Online guides can help you prepare, but they cannot replace your advisor.
