Welcome to Oxford, Ohio.

Classic brick streets, tree-lined parks, steamed bagels on High Street, and wooded trails just minutes from campus—here is everything freshmen and visiting families should know about Miami’s hometown.

storefront
SMALL TOWN, BIG SPIRIT

Where campus meets Uptown.

Located in southwestern Ohio about 35 miles northwest of Cincinnati, Oxford was founded alongside Miami University in 1809. High Street serves as the vibrant boundary between the historic red-brick campus and Uptown’s independent cafés, boutiques, and gathering spots.

Whether you are arriving for Summer Orientation, August Move-In, or Family Weekend, almost everything in Uptown is a pleasant five- to ten-minute walk from the Academic Quad.

QUICK TOWN FACTS

Oxford at a Glance

Walkability: Uptown High Street borders the north edge of campus—no car needed for daily errands or coffee runs.

THINGS TO DO

Local attractions & outdoor escapes.

Take a break from unpacking or campus tours with historic landmarks, weekend markets, and nearby state park trails.

park OUTDOOR ADVENTURE

Hueston Woods State Park

Just five miles north of campus, featuring Acton Lake, hiking and mountain biking trails, fossil hunting, canoe rentals, and a nature center.

Park details open_in_new
local_florist WEEKEND TRADITION

Oxford Farmers Market

Held Saturday mornings year-round near Main and Church Streets in Uptown, offering local produce, baked goods, honey, and handmade crafts.

Market hours open_in_new
museum LOCAL HISTORY

McGuffey House & Museum

The former home of Miami professor William Holmes McGuffey, creator of the famous 19th-century McGuffey Eclectic Readers, located right on Spring Street.

Museum info open_in_new
palette ARTS & CULTURE

Oxford Community Arts Center

Housed in the historic Oxford Female Institute building on South College Avenue, hosting rotating art galleries, concerts, and community workshops.

Events calendar open_in_new
terrain SCENIC TRAILS

Silvoor Biological Sanctuary

Part of Miami’s Natural Areas on the eastern edge of campus—quiet wooded paths and spring wildflowers ideal for a study break walk.

Uptown Parks & Yager Stadium

Catch summer concerts at Uptown Memorial Park Pavilion or cheer on Miami Football, Hockey, and Basketball during family visits.

WHERE TO EAT UPTOWN

Iconic Oxford restaurants & cafés.

Beyond campus dining halls, Uptown Oxford has decades-old student staples and family-friendly sit-down spots.

bakery_dining
E. HIGH STREET · SINCE 1975

Bagel & Deli Shop

Famous for more than 90 creative steamed bagel sandwiches—ask for the “Crunch & Munch” or “Miami Bagel” when parents are in town.

free_breakfast
W. SPRING STREET · BRUNCH FAVORITE

Patterson’s Cafe

Cozy family-owned breakfast and lunch spot serving pancakes, omelets, and fresh coffee all day just south of Uptown.

restaurant
E. HIGH STREET · CAMPUS STAPLE

Skipper’s Pub & Top Deck

A longtime Oxford tradition known for Greek-style gyros, burgers, and casual family lunches right across from campus.

lunch_dining
S. MAIN STREET · FAMILY DINNER

Gaslight Brewhouse

Hearty burgers, shareable appetizers, and family-friendly dinner seating in the heart of Uptown’s historic district.

local_pizza
E. HIGH STREET · LATE NIGHT & PIZZA

Bruno’s Pizza & SDS Pizza

Two beloved hometown pizzerias serving hot slices, subs, and classic “Bruno Dough” for move-in day fuel.

icecream
W. HIGH STREET · SWEET TREATS

Graeter’s Ice Cream & Fridge & Pantry

Grab fresh deli wraps at Fridge & Pantry on Church Street, then stroll to High Street for Ohio-famous French Pot ice cream.
