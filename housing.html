MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

Home is where your story grows.

Your residence hall is more than a room. It is where shared meals, late-night conversations, and new routines begin.

hotel
ROOM READY

Pack smart, feel at home.

Start with the basics. Check your residence hall’s rules and room details before buying large items.

  • Bring along
  • Coordinate
  • Check first

Things you will use

  • Bedding that matches your assigned mattress size
  • Towels, shower supplies, laundry bag and detergent
  • Laptop, chargers, notebooks and study tools
  • Everyday clothes, seasonal layers and a water bottle

Talk with your roommate

  • Shared cleaning and visitor expectations
  • Noise, lights and study hours
  • Optional shared items you both agree to bring
  • Contact preferences when one person is away

Verify the hall policies

  • Prohibited appliances and fire safety rules
  • Furniture dimensions and what is provided
  • Move-in instructions, dates and parking
  • Rules for adhesives, electrical items and decorations

LIFE TOGETHER

Good roommate habits go a long way.

chat_bubble_outline

Communicate early

Talk openly about routines and preferences. Small conversations prevent misunderstandings.

schedule

Respect different rhythms

Some people study late; others wake early. Agree on quiet times and shared expectations.

favorite_border

Make space for everyone

Be kind, respect belongings, and ask before borrowing or inviting guests.
