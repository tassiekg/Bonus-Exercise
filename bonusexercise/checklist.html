Before orientation

Start with the official steps and your university account.

Check for required tasks, deadlines and information tailored to you.
Make sure you can access your university email and key services.
Follow Miami’s official instructions for first-year students.
Check the requirements for your intended major and incoming credit.

Before move-in

Plan the practical details of living and learning at Miami.

Review your hall assignment, arrival guidance and housing messages.
Use the official Student Health Services information.
Think bedding, basic toiletries, school supplies and charging cables.
Explore the official campus map before the first day.

First week on campus

Make it personal — build a few routines and connections.

Explore meal-plan information and available options.
Save where to find advising, the library, or student support.
Attend something that matches your interests.
One short conversation can be the beginning of a friendship.
