MEET YOUR MIAMI

Your welcome starts here.

Orientation is the bridge between getting admitted and feeling like a RedHawk. Here is a friendly overview of what to expect.

celebration
FIND YOUR RHYTHM

A good start has three parts.

Miami’s official process includes an orientation Canvas course and an in-person program for incoming first-year students. Details depend on your entry term.

play_circle_outline

Before orientation

Check your portal, complete required online steps, and prepare questions for your advisor.

groups

During orientation

Meet other students, learn about campus life, discuss academics, and register for courses with guidance.

flag

After orientation

Review your schedule, explore campus resources, and get ready for your first weeks in Oxford.

ASK AWAY

Questions worth bringing.

  • help_outline What should I ask my advisor?
    Ask about major requirements, Miami Plan courses, placement credit, and balancing your first-semester schedule.
  • help_outline What if I do not know anyone?
    That is common. Try sitting with someone new, joining group activities, and saying hello to other first-year students.
  • help_outline Where do I find dates and requirements?
    Use your New Student Portal and Miami's official orientation page. Schedules can change by semester.
YOUR MINI MISSION

Try these three things.

  • Introduce yourself to three people.
  • Ask an advisor one thoughtful question.
  • Find a campus location you want to revisit.

Simple? Yes. Effective? Usually.
